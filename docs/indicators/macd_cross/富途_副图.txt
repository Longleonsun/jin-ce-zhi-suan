{MACD金叉死叉（副图）：白线DIF，黄线DEA，红绿柱为(DIF-DEA)*2；金叉处标B，死叉处标S
 参数在「参数设置」中定义：FAST 快线，SLOW 慢线，SIG 信号线（默认 3、6、5）}
DIF:EMA(C,FAST)-EMA(C,SLOW),COLORWHITE;
DEA:EMA(DIF,SIG),COLORYELLOW;
BAR:=(DIF-DEA)*2;
STICKLINE(BAR>=0,0,BAR,0.8,0),COLORRED;
STICKLINE(BAR<0,0,BAR,0.8,0),COLORGREEN;
DRAWTEXT(CROSS(DIF,DEA),DEA,'B'),COLORRED;
DRAWTEXT(CROSS(DEA,DIF),DEA,'S'),COLORGREEN;
