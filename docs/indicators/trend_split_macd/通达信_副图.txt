{趋势分离MACD
 上涨区（收盘价在MA50上方）：快速MACD(6,13,9)金叉买入，慢速MACD(24,52,9)死叉卖出
 下跌区（收盘价在MA50下方）：默认MACD(12,26,9)金叉买入，快速MACD处于死叉状态即卖出
 信号在K线收盘后确认，次日开盘执行}
{通达信·副图：红柱=持有，黄虚线=上涨区（收盘价在MA50上方）}
TUP:=(C>MA(C,50));
DIFF:=EMA(C,6)-EMA(C,13);
DEAF:=EMA(DIFF,9);
DIFM:=EMA(C,12)-EMA(C,26);
DEAM:=EMA(DIFM,9);
DIFS:=EMA(C,24)-EMA(C,52);
DEAS:=EMA(DIFS,9);
BUYSIG:=(TUP AND CROSS(DIFF,DEAF)) OR (NOT(TUP) AND CROSS(DIFM,DEAM));
SELLSIG:=(TUP AND CROSS(DEAS,DIFS)) OR (NOT(TUP) AND (DIFF<DEAF));
BB:=BARSLAST(BUYSIG);
SS:=BARSLAST(SELLSIG);
HOLD:=IF(SS>=0,BB<SS,BB>=0);
BPT:=HOLD AND (REF(HOLD,1)=0);
SPT:=(HOLD=0) AND (REF(HOLD,1)=1);
STICKLINE(HOLD,0,1,0.8,0),COLORRED;
HOLDING:IF(HOLD,1,0),COLORRED;
UPZONE:IF(TUP,0.5,0),COLORYELLOW,DOTLINE;
